PROJECT AND ARCHITECTURE
UNO Q Spirit is an engineering-preview instrument and a reference implementation for the broader Software-Defined Laboratory (SDL). The architecture separates physical sensor acquisition on the UNO Q STM32U585 MCU from Linux-side experiment logic, operator interface, archive, and analysis. Router Bridge carries status and commands between those sides. The wider SDL direction is a set of replaceable endpoints and reusable instruments serving versioned experiments, rather than a lab tied to one board.

LIMITATIONS AND SAFETY
- Dashboard is plain HTTP with no login; keep it on a trusted network.
- The original 5 V Spirit shield is not electrically qualified for UNO Q. Do not connect it directly; audit/modify the 5 V rails and signals, especially the ultrasonic ECHO line.
- Synthetic telemetry, host tests, source review, or compilation do not establish live sensor accuracy, calibration, or electrical safety.
- The AS7343 raw output slots are not calibrated wavelength/radiometric values; HX711 counts are not mass without calibration.
- Dashboard records telemetry; the complete autonomous laboratory described in the policy paper remains a proposal/roadmap. Physical actions need apparatus-specific risk review, local limits, human authorization, and tested abort behavior.
